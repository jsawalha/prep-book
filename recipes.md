# Frozen Soup Kit #1: Gochujang Jjigae
tags: freezer kit, soup, beef, korean
keeps: Freezer up to 3 months as a raw kit. Cook straight from frozen.

> Cut the zucchini, onion and tofu into roughly 1.5 cm (½ inch) cubes. Same-size pieces finish cooking at the same time as the thin beef.
> Lay the beef on top of the veggies in the container, not buried underneath, so it hits the boiling broth first and cooks through evenly.
> The broth isn't frozen with the kit. You add it at cook time, so the kit stays small in the freezer. A 1 L carton of bone broth is about 4 cups.
> Freezing makes tofu spongier and chewier. In a jjigae that's a plus, since it soaks up more broth.
> Boil until the beef is no longer pink and the zucchini is tender, usually 5 to 8 minutes after the broth returns to a full boil.
> Make it alongside Soup Kit #2: they share half a block of tofu each and 4 oz chuck each, so one block of tofu and 8 oz of beef covers both.

Macros per serving:

Protein: 38g
Carbs: 21g
Fat: 16g
Calories: 391

Ingredients per 2 servings (makes 2 portions of soup):

- 1 zucchini, whole
- 1 yellow onion, medium
- 1/2 block medium firm tofu
- 4 oz thinly sliced chuck steak
- 3 cups chicken bone broth
- 1 tbsp gochujang (korean red chili paste)
- 1/2 tbsp gochugaru (korean spicy chilli flakes)
- 1 tbsp doenjang (fermented soybean paste)
- 1 tbsp minced garlic
- 1 tbsp soy sauce

How to make it yourself:

1. Chop your zucchini, onion, and tofu into small cubes.
2. In a small bowl, mix together gochujang, gochugaru, doenjang, minced garlic, and soy sauce to make your broth sauce.
3. In a meal prep container, add your chopped veggies, broth sauce, and meat.
4. Seal the container and freeze overnight or until ready to eat.
5. When ready to eat, remove the lid & microwave the container for 15 seconds to make it easy to remove the frozen block of ingredients.
6. In a pot on high heat, add your frozen block if ingredients and chicken bone broth and cover with a lid as it comes to a boil.
7. Boil until everything is cooked through and enjoy!

# One-Pot Hainanese Chicken Rice
tags: rice cooker, chicken
keeps: Fridge 3–4 days. Chili sauce keeps about a week on its own.

> Step 1 mentions green onions, but they aren't in the ingredient list. Add 1–2 if you want them on top.
> Rinse the rice until the water runs mostly clear. Rice cooked with chicken juices gets gummy if it isn't washed.
> Thighs reheat much better than breast. The chicken is done at 74°C (165°F) in the thickest piece. If it's short, run a few more minutes on keep-warm or a second quick cycle.
> Slice the chicken after it's cooked, not before, so it stays juicy.
> Pack the chili sauce and cucumbers in separate small containers so the rice doesn't go soggy.
> Scaling up: don't fill past your rice cooker's max line. In a typical 5.5-cup cooker, about 3x (2 cups raw rice plus the chicken) is the practical limit.

Macros per 1 serving:

Protein: 49g
Carbs: 63g
Fat: 12g
Calories: 583

Ingredients per 2 servings:

Chicken & Rice

- 14 oz chicken thighs, skinless, boneless, raw
- 1.5 tsp sesame oil
- 1/4 tsp salt
- 1.5 tbsp minced garlic
- 1 tbsp minced ginger
- 1 cup chicken bone broth
- 2/3 cups jasmine rice, uncooked/raw
- 1 tsp chicken bouillon powder
- 1 persian cucumber

Chili Sauce

- 1.5 tbsp minced garlic
- 2 tbsp sambal chili paste
- 2 tbsp sriracha
- 2 tbsp chicken broth
- 1 tbsp lime juice
- 1/2 tsp brown sugar

How to make it yourself:

1. Dice your cucumbers into thin circles and thinly slice your green onions.
2. Wash your uncooked rice clean.
3. In your rice cooker pot, add your minced garlic, minced ginger, chicken bone broth, chicken bouillon powder, and uncooked rice and mix
4. Marinate your raw chicken with salt and sesame oil then add into your rice cooker pot.
5. Cook in your rice cooker for 30-35 minutes on your rice cooker's quick cook setting.
6. In a small bowl, mix together minced garlic, sambal chili paste, sriracha, chicken broth, lime juice, and brown sugar to make your sauce.
7. Once cooked, plate half your chicken, half your rice, half the chili sauce, and half your cucumbers and enjoy!

# Kimchi Tuna Pancakes (Kimchi Jeon)
tags: cook fresh, tuna, korean
keeps: Best fresh. Cooked pancakes keep 2 days in the fridge.

> Older, sour kimchi has the most flavor. Snip it into small pieces with kitchen scissors right in the bowl.
> Drain the tuna well and squeeze some liquid out of the kimchi, or the batter turns gummy. Swap a splash of the kimchi juice in for some of the water for more flavor.
> Use ice-cold water in the batter. Cold batter fries up crispier.
> Thinner pancakes are crispier. Spread the batter to about 1 cm (⅓ inch) thick, and don't press on it while it cooks.
> This one isn't great as a meal prep. If you want to prep ahead, mix everything except the flour and water, and stir those in right before cooking.
> Reheat leftovers in a dry pan or air fryer, not the microwave, to get the crunch back.

Macros per serving:

Protein: 42g
Carbs: 84g
Fat: 4g
Calories: 536

Ingredients per 2 servings (make around 2-4 jeon depending on pan size):

- 4 cups kimchi
- 1.5 cups flour
- 1.5 cups water
- 1 jalapeno, diced
- salt to taste
- 1 cup green onion
- 10 oz light canned tuna, in water
- 1 tbsp gochugaru
- 1 second spray oil

How to make it yourself:

1. Finely slice your green onions and jalapenos.
2. In a large bowl, add your green onions, jalapenos, cut kimchi, flour, water, canned tuna, gochugaru, and salt and mix well to create a batter like consistency.
3. In a pan on medium heat coated with spray oil, add your batter and spread it thin to create a thin pancake until the bottom starts to crisp and brown and the pancake moves freely when you shake the pan.
4. Flip using a paper plate to cook the other side until scripy brown on both sides and enjoy!

# Pad Kra Pao Turkey Rice Bowl
tags: rice cooker, turkey, thai
keeps: Fridge 3–4 days. Fry the eggs fresh.

> Traditional pad kra pao uses holy basil. Thai basil is the usual substitute, and Italian basil works in a pinch.
> Two bird's eye chilies is properly spicy. Seed them, or use one, for milder heat.
> Ground turkey needs to reach 74°C (165°F). After cooking, break up the meat and check the center of the clump. If it's still pink, run another short cycle.
> Fry the eggs right before you eat; it takes 2 minutes. Reheated fried eggs turn rubbery.
> Pack the diced cucumber separately and add it cold after reheating.

Macros per serving:

Protein: 61g
Carbs: 62g
Fat: 8g
Calories: 585

Ingredients per serving (makes 2 servings):

- 12 oz ground turkey, raw
- 1 tbsp fish sauce
- 1 tbsp soy sauce
- 1 tbsp oyster sauce
- 1 cup Thai basil
- 1 tbsp minced garlic
- 2 whole Thai bird eye chili peppers
- 2/3 cup uncooked jasmine rice
- 1 onion, whole
- 2 handfuls of spinach
- 1/3 cup chicken bone broth

[Plating]

- 2 eggs
- 1/3 second spray oil
- 2 large Persian cucumbers

How to make it yourself:

1. In a bowl, mix together ground turkey with soy sauce, fish sauce, oyster sauce, minced garlic, Thai basil, and diced Thai chili.
2. Wash your rice.
3. In a rice cooker, add your marinated ground turkey, a diced onion, spinach, chicken bone broth, and your washed rice and cook for 25 minutes in your rice cooker.
4. Dice your Persian cucumber.
5. In a pan coated with spray oil, fry two sunny side up eggs season with salt and pepper to taste.
6. Once done, remove half of the turkey, rice, and veggies, and plate alongside 1 jammy egg and your diced Persian cucumbers and enjoy!

# Frozen Soup Kit #2: Hot Pot
tags: freezer kit, soup, beef, korean
keeps: Freezer up to 3 months as a raw kit. Cook straight from frozen.

> Make it alongside Soup Kit #1: same beef, same tofu block, same freezer method.
> Cut the thick white napa stems smaller than the leafy tops so they soften at the same time.
> Enoki: cut off the spongy root base, then pull the mushrooms apart into small bunches.
> If your rice cakes are hard (refrigerated or frozen), soak them in water for 10 minutes before packing so they cook evenly.
> Bean sprouts and enoki go soft after freezing. That's fine in soup, but if you want crunch, keep them in a bag in the fridge and add them at boil time (use within 3 days).

Macros per serving:

Protein: 38g
Carbs: 40g
Fat: 16g
Calories: 453

Ingredients per 2 servings (makes 2 portions of soup):

- 40g bean sprouts
- 4 large leaves of napa cabbage (approximately 60g)
- 4 oz thinly sliced chuck steak
- 1/2 block medium firm tofu
- 1/2 cup sliced korean rice cakes
- 30g enoki mushrooms
- 3 cup beef bone broth
- 1 tbsp minced garlic
- 1 tbsp honey
- 2 tbsp soy sauce
- 2 tsp black pepper
- salt to taste

How to make it yourself:

1. Chop your napa cabbage into chunks and tofu into small cubes.
2. In a small bowl, mix together minced garlic, honey, soy sauce, black pepper, and salt to make your broth sauce.
3. In a meal prep container, add half your chopped cabbage, broth sauce, meat, tofu, Korean rice cakes, bean sprouts, and enoki mushrooms.
4. Seal the container and freeze overnight or until ready to eat.
5. When ready to eat, remove the lid & microwave the container for 15 seconds to make it easy to remove the frozen block of ingredients.
6. In a pot on high heat, add your frozen block of ingredients and beef bone broth and cover with a lid as it comes to a boil.
7. Boil until everything is cooked through and enjoy!

# One-Pot Miso Salmon Rice Bowl
tags: rice cooker, salmon, japanese
keeps: Fridge 2–3 days. Eat this one first in the week.

> Salmon is the most fragile leftover here. Eat it first in the week, and reheat at 50% power so the fish doesn't dry out.
> Salmon is done at 63°C (145°F), or when it flakes easily with a fork.
> Miso and soy are both salty. Low-sodium soy sauce keeps it in check.
> 10 napa leaves looks like a lot but cooks way down. Chop it bite-size.
> Beech mushrooms: cut off the base and separate them by hand.
> Fry the eggs fresh when you eat.

Macros per serving:

Protein: 40g
Carbs: 63g
Fat: 25g
Calories: 659

Ingredients per serving (makes 2 servings):

[Marinade]

- 2.5 tbsp miso paste
- 3 tbsp soy sauce
- 1 tbsp mirin
- 1 tsp sesame oil
- 1 tbsp minced garlic

[Rice Cooker]

- 2/3 cups jasmine rice, uncooked/raw
- 1/3 cup beef bone broth
- 7 oz atlantic farm raised salmon, skinless, raw
- 10 large napa cabbage leaves
- 1/2 cup peeled edamame
- 1 cup white beech mushrooms

[Topping]

- 2 pan-fried eggs
- salt and pepper to taste
- 1/3 second spray oil
- 2 green onions

How to make it yourself:

1. Chop your napa cabbage leaves and green onions into small pieces.
2. In a bowl mix together miso paste, soy sauce, mirin, sesame oil, and minced garlic to make your marinade .
3. Wash your rice.
4. In your rice cooker add your washed rice, beef bone broth, salmon covered with the marinade, napa cabbage, edamame, and white beech mushrooms and cook for 20 minutes.
5. In a pan coated with spray oil, fry two sunny side up eggs season with salt and pepper to taste .
6. Once cooked mix together the rice with a spoon, take out half a portion of the mixed salmon rice, add one of the eggs, and top it off with half of the green onions and enjoy!

> Wash and dry the lettuce, then store it wrapped in a paper towel inside a bag. Wrap the bowls when you eat, not when you prep.

# Garlic Tofu Veggie Rice
tags: rice cooker, tofu, vegetarian, big batch
servings: 5
keeps: Fridge 4–5 days. Freezes well for about 2 months.

> The macros below are my estimates (the original recipe didn't list any): about 2.5 cups raw jasmine rice, a 14 oz block of extra-firm tofu, and the veg and sauce, split 5 ways. At 4 servings, multiply by 1.25.
> This is the lowest-protein recipe in the book. Add a fried egg or a second block of tofu if it's a main meal.
> 2.25 cups of liquid for 2.5 cups of rice is on the dry side; the veggies and sauce make up the difference. If your cooker runs dry or the bottom crusts, go to 2.5 cups next time.
> Press the tofu for at least 15 minutes (wrap it in a towel under something heavy) so it doesn't water down the rice.
> Frozen corn and edamame go straight in. No need to thaw.
> 2.5 cups raw rice plus toppings is near the limit of a 5.5-cup rice cooker. Stay under the max line.
> Don't stir after layering the veggies and tofu. Stirring before cooking makes the rice gummy.

Macros per serving:

Protein: 19g
Carbs: 90g
Fat: 11g
Calories: 540

Ingredients (makes 5 servings):

[Rice Cooker]

- 2.5 cups jasmine or long-grain white rice, uncooked/raw
- 2.25 cups water or vegetable broth
- 1 block extra-firm tofu
- 3/4 cup shelled edamame
- 3/4 cup carrots, finely diced
- 3/4 cup sweet corn
- 1 cup red and yellow bell peppers, diced

[Garlic-Sugar Sauce]

- 3.5 tbsp soy sauce
- 1 tbsp sugar (white or brown)
- 4 cloves garlic, finely minced (or 2 tsp garlic powder)
- 2 tbsp sesame oil
- 1/2 tsp white or black pepper

How to make it yourself:

1. Press your tofu dry and cut it into 1/2 inch cubes.
2. Rinse your rice under cold water until the water runs clear. Drain well and add it to your rice cooker.
3. In a measuring cup or bowl, whisk together your water or broth, soy sauce, sugar, minced garlic, sesame oil, and pepper until the sugar dissolves. Pour it over the rice and stir.
4. Spread the carrots, corn, bell peppers, and edamame evenly on top of the rice. Don't stir.
5. Spread the tofu cubes in an even layer on top of the veggies.
6. Close the lid and cook on the white rice setting.
7. When it's done, keep the lid shut for 10 more minutes so the center finishes steaming.
8. Gently fold from the bottom up with a rice paddle to mix in the sauce and veggies, then serve.
